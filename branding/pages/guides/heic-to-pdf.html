Convert HEIC to PDF (Free, No Upload)

iPhones save photos as .heic, but most application forms, HR portals and printers expect a PDF. Here's the fastest fix — done entirely in your browser, so the photos (passport scans, payslips, ID cards) never touch a server.

Steps

  1. Open the HEIC to PDF tool.
  2. Select one or more .heic files from your phone or computer.
  3. Drag to reorder pages if needed (multi-photo selection is supported).
  4. Click Convert — you get a single PDF with one photo per page, in order.
  5. Download. If the resulting PDF is too large for a portal limit, run it through the Compress PDF tool with a KB target.

Common cases

  • Job applications: convert photo + ID + certificates, then merge everything with the Merge PDF tool into one file.
  • Rent agreements or receipts: convert, merge, then compress under a size limit.
  • Printing: HEIC keeps live-photo metadata that some printers refuse — the PDF cleans that up.

Is HEIC exclusive to Apple?

No — Android 10+ and many cameras also write .heic. The converter handles JPG, PNG, BMP, GIF, TIFF, WebP, JP2/JPX (JPEG 2000) and more, so it doubles as a general image-to-PDF converter.
