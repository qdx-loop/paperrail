Merge PDF Files Without Uploading Them

Scanned a 20-page contract as separate files? Or a bank statement exported page by page? Merging fixes that — and doing it in your browser means the contract, tax document or medical report is never sent to anyone's server.

Steps

  1. Open the Merge PDF tool.
  2. Select all the PDFs you want combined (or drag them in).
  3. Drag the file cards to set the page order.
  4. Click Merge and download the single PDF.
  5. Too big for an email or portal? Compress it to a target size afterwards.

When merging on a server is risky

Merge tools that work "in the cloud" receive your documents at full fidelity and store a copy during processing. For anything you wouldn't email to a stranger — ID scans, leases, payslips, medical records — a local merge is the better default. PaperRail runs the engine in your browser with WebAssembly; the upload pipe simply doesn't exist.

Alternatives inside PaperRail

  • Alternate merge — interleave two scans page by page (duplex scanning recovery).
  • Split PDF — the opposite problem: one huge file into sections.
  • Delete pages — drop blanks or junk pages while you're at it.
